FONTLOG for the Collab Sans font family

This file provides detailed information on the Collab Sans Font Software.
This information should be distributed along with the Collab Sans fonts
and any derivative works.

Basic Font Information
----------------------
Collab Sans is a rounded neo-grotesque typeface family derived from Rasmus
Andersson's open-source typeface "Inter". Collab Sans features tangent-continuous
semicircular stroke terminals (R = 0.50 * W), adaptive sub-contour scaling for
diacritics and punctuation, and ink-trap preserved concave fillets (R = 0.14 * W)
across all 18 styles (9 weights from Thin to Black, with matching true italics).

Collab Sans was created by Yugal with Antigravity to establish a warm, friendly,
and minimalist brand identity complementing the "Collab" ecosystem (https://yugal.one)
while maintaining the legendary optical clarity and comprehensive glyph coverage of Inter.

ChangeLog
---------
2026-11-01 (Yugal) Collab Sans Version 1.000
- Initial open-source release as Collab Sans.
- Derived from Inter with complete geometric rounding engine built with Antigravity.
- First open-source release of Collab Sans with Reserved Font Name "Collab Sans"
  to ensure full trademark compliance and Google Fonts readiness.
- 18 styles generated in TrueType (.ttf), OpenType CFF (.otf), and WOFF2 (.woff2).
- Full glyph coverage of 2,548 glyphs with complete OpenType layout features
  (GPOS, GSUB, kerning, tnum, cv01-cv11, ss01-ss04).
